# Feature-pruning audit

**Plausible but unverified:** design choices. “Keep” means retain intent, not implemented. Evaluate each feature by board depth (B), corporate competition (C), finance decisions (F), agency (A), progression/replay (P) and manageable interface (U). Criteria are qualitative filters, not invented measured scores.

|Feature|Decision|Value / required simplification or evidence|
|---|---|---|
|One exceptional central board game|Blocked / RESEARCH STOP|No R0 candidate selected; diagnose one A repetition before further invention or production|
|Five old games as release modes|Reject|Fragmented mastery; retain as research controls only|
|Larger boards/more piece types|Defer|B unknown; fixed-reserve 5/6/7 controls remain small; raw branching never proves depth|
|Local difficulty/ratings/replay|Keep|B/P/U; measured budget ladder and uncertainty, no superhuman claim|
|Actual positions/reporting|Keep|C/A/P; show local scope, not all seats simultaneously|
|Apply/compete/approval/promote|Improve|C/A/P; one-button application and published appointment contract|
|Tournament for every vacancy|Simplify|C/U; small paired trials; background recruitment handles routine posts|
|Salary/prizes and real funding|Keep|F/P; one ledger, no decorative balances|
|Roster/training/budget authority|Keep|B/C/A; delegate routine policy with caps|
|Chair/CEO/ownership distinction|Keep later|C/F/A; governance test before adding all roles|
|Research departments/tools|Improve|F/A/P; public data only; horizontal tracks, diminishing returns|
|Binary third-party markets|Defer to validated lifecycle|F/A; optional, bounded, conflict-safe|
|Own-match betting with campaign funds|Reject|Destroys competitive/economic integrity|
|Sandbox market|Defer|A; isolated nonconvertible balance, do not distract first slice|
|Career/CEO/earnings/acquisition markets|Defer|F; influence/settlement unresolved|
|Investing/dividends/ownership|Keep staged|F/A/P; cap tables and cash flows before valuation sophistication|
|Founding/acquisition/holding companies|Defer|C/F/A; late scope, acyclic ownership initially|
|Permanent notable history/rivalries|Keep|C/P; event-backed summaries and bounded archive growth|
|20,000 staff at launch|Reject as requirement|C/P needs measured relevance; grow from 18/120/2k fixtures|
|Unbounded full-match AI every day|Reject|Cost unjustified; detail-lock important matches and disclose approximations|
|Four destinations/header|Keep|U/P; remove separate people/company dashboards|
|Pixel offices/portraits/trophies|Simplify|P/U; bounded authored kit and milestones|
|Office decorating/collectible grind|Reject|No core decision benefit|
|Decorative XP/daily chores/HR forms|Reject|Adds friction without meaningful agency|
|Real-time waits/login rewards|Reject|Conflicts offline deliberate-time sessions|
|Paid APIs/accounts/remote inference|Reject|Violates product requirements|
|Tamper-proof local saves/markets|Reject as promise|Device owner can inspect data; prioritize corruption recovery|
|Export/import/migrations/accessibility|Keep|A/U; essential trust and usability, not polish deferred forever|

Final R0 rejects advancing A/B/C as product rules; A is a diagnostic lead only. A zero-draw challenger does not earn advancement when deeper skill separation and intended victory behavior are unsupported. Keep the civilization ambitions, defer their construction.

Revisit deferred mechanics only after their dependency gate in [roadmap](LEVERAGE_IMPLEMENTATION_ROADMAP.md). Every added feature must replace complexity elsewhere or justify its interaction with the main loop. No feature is complete because a screen displays its name.
