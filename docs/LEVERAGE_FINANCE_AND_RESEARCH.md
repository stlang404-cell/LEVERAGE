# Finance and research intelligence

**Plausible but unverified:** product design. **Verified within limited tests:** historical LMSR numeric samples and cash transfers only. **Experimentally supported:** synthetic forecast comparisons in `world_bench.json`; not board-game calibration or proof of profit.

## Power unlocks tools, not privileged outcomes

All official financial research uses the same immutable public data snapshot: published match histories, uncertain ratings, public lineups and disclosed finances. Rank changes the tools, workflow and compute allocation, never access to a future move, committed random seed, hidden match result or private engine evaluation while a market is open. Research staff do not run the board engine on open contracts. Public pre-match statistical model fitting is permitted and uncertain; private move analysis remains locked until settlement. Earlier blanket analysis-lock language is narrowed to distinguish public statistics from prohibited engine/future information.

|Access / specialization|Tool and actual decision|Cost / constraint|
|---|---|---|
|Entry|Ratings, past matches, price history, baseline probability range|Free public view; no forecast certainty|
|Analyst / Finance|Compare public trends, calibration bins and fees|One research task; cost paid to staff budget|
|Manager / Operations|Public lineup scouting and opponent-style summaries|Limited assistant slots; no covert lineup changes|
|Director / Strategy|Tournament scenario tree and model comparison|Disclosed independence assumptions; compare sensitivity|
|VP / Finance|Portfolio exposure, correlated drawdown and valuation scenarios|Risk limits still bind; no extra collateral from status|
|Executive / Research|Model validation, backtesting and model-drift alerts|Holdout data mandatory; diminishing gains|
|CEO/chair / Leadership|Company research priorities and capital allocation|Corporate funds remain corporate; governance conflicts exclude related contracts|

Department tracks are horizontal: Finance improves accounting/risk tools; Strategy focuses brackets; Research validates models; Operations analyzes staffing/capacity; Leadership governs allocation. An independent investor can buy equivalent lawful public-statistics analysis using personal funds, preserving a non-executive route. A skilled junior may use free tools better than an executive. Do not charge players to reveal basic risk, fees or conflicts.

Synthetic experiment: 5,000 outcomes from a logistic latent rating gap, forecasts from averages of 1/4/8/16/32 noisy public observations, nested identical observations for paired comparisons. Lower Brier is better; calibration bins in raw output expose overconfidence. This is intentionally a controlled measurement of averaging, NOT evidence that ranked research will predict this game's engine. Costs equal observation count solely as a toy effort model. Real adoption requires rolling out-of-time public matches, calibration slope/intercept, Brier/log loss, confidence bands and net return after fees. Tune shrinkage on training data, not the held-out outcomes. More compute may reduce error on average but cannot guarantee any bet or profit. Freeze data/model version at contract cutoff.

## Market lifecycle and categories

Retain [financial foundation](../research/10_PREDICTION_MARKETS_AND_FINANCE.md): binary long-only initial contracts, no debt/shorts, funded LMSR subsidy b·ln(2), disclosed fees, cash-backed position limits, lock → resolve → idempotent settle; void refunds trades/fees by declared policy. Integer microcurrency journal plus a specified deterministic cost approximation before launch. Maker subsidy and deposits remain reserved, not available for unrelated payroll. No markets enabled until full lifecycle tests pass; those tests have NOT been implemented in this continuation.

|Category|Decision|
|---|---|
|Independent third-party match|Keep, after lifecycle and information-flow gates|
|Tournament winner|Defer multi-outcome/correlated liabilities until binary works|
|Promotions / CEO succession|Defer; eligibility/governance and player influence complicate resolution|
|Earnings, mergers, survival|Defer until corporate accounting/valuation and conflicts are proven|
|Own/controlled match|Sandbox only; separate nonconvertible funds, no career rewards|

Exclude any causal influence: self, supervised people, controlled companies, teams whose staffing/lineup the player can change, or investments granting meaningful governance rights. Conservative proposal: any board seat or ≥5% voting stake triggers review/exclusion; threshold is unvalidated and causality can exclude below it. If authority changes during trading, freeze and void under posted policy rather than exploit the window. Public data and market odds remain visible to excluded players; trading does not.

## Wealth and recovery

Cash conservation journal: salary/prize/dividend paid by a funded source; explicit scenario issuance account for any recovery subsidy. Shares and claims are distinct from cash. Net worth = spendable + restricted cash + marked equity/claims − debt, with illiquidity disclosed and no double counting at consolidated holding companies. Marked gains do not finance spending. Dividends reduce treasury and ex-dividend value. Issuance dilutes, acquisitions transfer existing assets, bankruptcy pays declared priority. Cap-table and settlement invariants are prerequisites to investing/control features.

Preserve no-betting career path: salaries, free competitions, funded prizes, lawful ownership and research. No real money, deposits, cash-out, paid currency or compulsory wagering. Local files can be inspected; offline tamper-proof markets are a rejected promise. Save integrity protects against corruption, not the device owner.
