# LEVERAGE — product vision

**Plausible but unverified:** proposed product experience. These are design commitments, not achieved features. LEVERAGE is the official product title. CORPORATE LADDER appears only in preserved historical research; frozen technical versions remain unchanged.

Begin as an unknown intern. Build mastery, win genuine selection competitions, lead people, accumulate ownership or become an independent investor. Salary, job authority, rating and net worth can diverge. A junior can defeat a CEO under identical board rules; an owner can be a mediocre player; a champion may remain an individual contributor.

The central loop is **apply → compete → approval → promote**, with practice, investing and rival-company careers as alternatives. The central game must earn repeated play without the corporation or betting. Consequences matter through funded salaries, vacancies, reputation, meaningful authority and persistent rivals, not arbitrary rank XP.

## Product contracts

- One published deterministic perfect-information board ruleset per official competition; career grade never alters it. Research selectors are not extra release minigames.
- Arena first; four destinations only: Arena, Exchange, Career, Empire. Few mandatory words, contextual explanations and accessible previews.
- Premium bounded pixel-art kit; distinct silhouettes, readable type and optional reduced motion.
- Local single-player, no accounts, no paid APIs, no cloud inference, no cash-out or purchasable wagering currency. Play offline after installation.
- Windows installer and iPhone PWA are targets requiring separate tests. Save recovery/export are essential gameplay features.
- Loss never removes practice or every recovery route. No compulsory gambling, daily chores, real waiting or promotion treadmill.

**Rejected:** shipping a corporation around an unvalidated game. **Speculative:** exceptional depth, premium visual quality, calibrated strong AI and 20,000-person living histories; these require empirical gates. See [roadmap](LEVERAGE_IMPLEMENTATION_ROADMAP.md).
