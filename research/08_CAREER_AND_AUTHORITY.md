# Career, competition and authority

## Distinct signals

Match rating estimates board skill, with uncertainty; it never equals grade or wealth. Performance review is public weighted evidence: 50% normalized match contribution, 30% team operational objective, 20% reliability. This weighting is an initial tuning proposal. Normalize board contribution against expected score to prevent farming weak opponents. Team performance cannot be invented solely from wins: staffing and training budgets constrain service output.

Eligibility requires minimum tenure of one review cycle, training qualification and no active budget violation. Advertised openings rank eligible applicants on review evidence; final review records reasons and permits appeals/transfers. No promotion without a genuine vacancy or approved new position. Executive contests influence the shortlist; a single upset never automatically hands an intern a corporation. Underperformance first causes a warning and recovery objective; two failed reviews permit demotion subject to charter, notice and paid obligations. CEO succession follows board vote, not the manager promotion formula.

## Authority matrix (all proposals)

|Role|Real actions|Limits and consequences|
|---|---|---|
|Employee|Play, train, apply, resign, invest|Time allocation; salary depends on funded seat|
|Team leader|Choose roster/training, recommend hire|Cannot spend beyond team envelope; players may opt out of personal matches|
|Manager|Approve team hiring, budgets and eligible team promotions|Needs vacancy and funding; conflicts recorded|
|Director|Reorganize departments and redistribute envelopes|Severance cost and capacity disruption forecast before commitment|
|VP|Set division objectives, propose acquisitions|Board approvals above thresholds|
|CEO|Allocate corporation envelopes, propose financing and strategy|Treasury constraints, charter, board dismissal|
|Chair|Agenda, CEO appointment process, board policy votes|One vote unless charter says otherwise; not unilateral treasury control|
|Controlling owner|Elect board under voting rights; propose governance|No direct access to corporate cash; minority rights and existing contracts|

Delegate routine choices to explicit policies with caps, stop conditions and a decision log. Weekly dashboard shows at most three urgent choices; optional drill-down gives full organization. Administrative actions should change tournament resources, staffing or future opportunities. If a promotion adds clicks without new agency, redesign it.

Recovery: demotion preserves rating, reputation history and vested shares; layoffs preserve eligibility elsewhere; unpaid employees can take guaranteed small public match stipends funded by a declared civic sponsor. Debt cannot prevent playing or accessing training. Practice and career tournaments have identical board rules. Prestige is separate from wealth and position.
