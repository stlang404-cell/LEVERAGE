# Risk register and final quality audit

Status vocabulary: Verified = directly checked property in stated scope; Experimentally Supported = sampled evidence; Plausible but Unverified = design argument; Speculative = uncertain scale/experience; Rejected = intentionally excluded.

|Risk / audit question|Status and evidence|Mitigation or next experiment|
|---|---|---|
|Do exact rules execute?|Verified within harness: legal transitions, terminal fixtures, invariants; 11 tests|Independent TS oracle and exhaustive reduced states|
|Originality|Plausible but Unverified; combinations proposed, not novelty cleared|Broader source survey and designer review|
|Clear teaching and victory|Plausible but Unverified; exact definitions exist|Teach-back with 12 testers; identify dual-victory errors|
|Repeated-play strategic depth|Unknown; Speculative|Deeper agents, positional ablation, human repeated sessions|
|Leverage repetition|Experimentally Supported: 13/48 draws|Connectivity variants, stronger defenses, endgame tablebase|

|Career meaning and demotions|Plausible but Unverified|Vertical-slice choice observation and no-betting campaign|
|Economic coherence|Verified only algebra/sample transfer examples; full system unimplemented|Ledger invariants, adversarial lifecycle tests and long-run simulation|
|Local market insider conflicts|Plausible rules, not tested|Authority closure tests and information-flow audit|
|Tamper-proof local economy|Rejected as promise|Transparent single-player policy and sandbox boundaries|

|Offline Windows/PWA|Plausible architecture, not built|Windows installer and physical iPhone spikes|

|Source freshness|Unverified externally; links not freshly retrieved|Verify primary rules, publication metadata and platform docs before freezing specification|

No production readiness claim follows from this package. The scientific result is an executable starting point plus evidence against premature certainty. Scope is realistic only as staged development with a board-game rejection gate.
