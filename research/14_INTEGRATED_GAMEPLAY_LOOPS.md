# Ten complete player journeys (proposed, not observed)

Each row explicitly links choice → board → organization → money → AI response → next option. Every competition uses the same rules. No portfolio or title buys board power.

|Journey|Choice and board consequence|Organization and money|AI reaction and next option|
|---|---|---|---|
|Intern → manager|Choose training over an extra paid exhibition; beat expected tournament score|Review qualifies for a posted team-lead vacancy; salary rises only after funded appointment|Displaced rival applies elsewhere; lead a roster or stay an individual specialist. Manager requires a later genuine vacancy|
|Manager → executive|Develop junior roster and enter division championship|Team service output and match contribution open VP shortlist; bonus from divisional treasury|Other applicants campaign on budget performance; negotiate role or pursue competitive title|
|Executive demoted|Ignore training to overexpand; repeated poor results trigger warnings|Two failed reviews yield board-approved lower seat, reduced future salary, retained vested equity|Successor recruits strong staff; accept recovery objective or transfer|
|Fired employee joins rival|Resign after dismissal review, win open qualifier|Rival vacancy offer funds new salary; old restricted market exposure freezes by policy|Former teammates become competitors; build rivalry without erasing history|
|Investor without office|Keep accessible entry job or public exhibitions, play selected matches for mastery|Save wages, diversify equity; returns depend on funded dividends and valuation|Companies recruit based on public reputation; buy stake or remain independent|
|CEO defends position|Choose training/roster over short-term payout; play succession tournament|Board evaluates both contest and operations; defeat alone does not force removal, repeated failure raises vote risk|Directors may back successor; disclose turnaround or negotiate retirement|
|Found rival corporation|Fund charter and staff, compete in public company league|Capital moves into distinct treasury; payroll can bankrupt venture|Recruitment offers alter AI loyalty; grow sustainably or sell control|
|Controlling owner challenges CEO|Acquire voting stake with personal cash, avoid affected markets|Elect board within charter; company cash remains separate|CEO solicits other holders; replace leadership through vote or accept reform|
|Low-rated worker upsets CEO|Choose risky bridge attack, earn a genuine board victory|Rating and reputation improve with uncertainty; tournament prize paid, no automatic executive appointment|CEO trains for rematch; worker gets interview or higher league invitation|
|Ruined player rebuilds|Stop betting, review losses, play free league/sponsor matches|Small funded stipend restarts savings; no forced loan or negative playable access|Recruiters value recent results; return to employment, invest cautiously or pursue pure competition|

## Loop audit

Rating farming: cap same-opponent rated repetition and normalize opponent expectation. Promotion farming: vacancy, funding and review cycles gate moves. Authority/market manipulation: causal conflict exclusion and void/refund policy. Infinite subsidiary valuation: no control cycles and consolidated asset accounting. Forced management chores: explicit delegation caps. Betting necessity: run a full no-betting campaign gate. Administrative time gates are simulated days, never real waiting. Financial recovery sponsor has a declared scenario budget/issuance policy; do not silently mint bonuses.

Tournament pressure should arise from bracket stakes and public reviews, not compulsory knockouts from the whole campaign. Losing a cup removes the player from that bracket but preserves practice, employment applications and future events. Optional prestige “elimination season” can be a mode only after recovery paths work.
